## Scanned pages

The scanned Khojki pages (`sample_khojki_script_vatiations.pdf`) are **not** included
in this repository. They were provided for this exercise only. The repository contains
five small line crops from page 18 (`data/gt_lines/*.png`) and pipeline output images
derived from the scans (`results/`), kept so the evaluation can be checked. They are
included for review of this exercise only and should not be reused.

## Synthetic text

`data/corpus/corpus.txt` contains generated Khojki strings and Gujarati text converted
to Khojki with Aksharamukha. Sources of the Gujarati text are listed in the corpus
header.
